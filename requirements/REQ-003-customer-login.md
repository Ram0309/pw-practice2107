# REQ-003 Customer login

As a registered customer, I want to log in with my email and password so that I can see my account and orders.

Acceptance criteria:
- A registered customer with a valid email and password is logged in and sees "Log out" in the header.
- An unknown email shows "Login was unsuccessful. Please correct the errors and try again."
- An invalid email format shows "Please enter a valid email address."
- The login page should be secure.
