# REQ-002 Add product to shopping cart

As a guest shopper, I want to add a product to my shopping cart from the search results so that I can buy it later.

Acceptance criteria:
- Clicking "Add to cart" on a product tile shows "The product has been added to your shopping cart".
- The header cart counter increases from (0) to (1).
- The shopping cart page lists the added product with quantity 1.
- The cart line total equals unit price multiplied by quantity.
